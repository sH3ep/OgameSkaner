Miner

The miner class is indispensable for empires that want to maximize resource production on their planets.
  • +50% Resource production (Affects all bonuses.)
  • 2x speed for cargo ships. (Between the planets you own)

Commander

The Commander class is the best choice for empires that want to grow their empires with the spoils of war and to dominate their rivals in wars.
  • +50% speed for warships and recyclers.
  • 30% ship repair after battles (only attacker)
  • -25% fuel consumption.
  • +10 Fleet slots
  • +2 Espionage level
  • +2 extra research levels for Weapon, Shield, Armor, Laser, Ion, Plasma, Gravitation technologies.
  • 2x Saved planets

Discoverer

You can enrich your empire with discoveries made to the deepest and unknown points of the universe. The discoverer class would be a good choice for such curious empires.
  • Ability to do 40-minute expedition missions. (1 hour minimum for other classes)
  • 20% debris field in battles with aliens. (15% for other classes)
  • 30% more resource in expedition missions.
